## Ceramic Coating Protection

A ceramic coating bonds to your clear coat and shields the paint from UV, ash, road grime, bird droppings and tree sap for years, where wax or sealant wears off in months. Every coating comes with a full warranty, Carfax integration and included maintenance.

You see the difference right away: water and dirt bead up and roll off, the paint has a deeper gloss, and everyday contaminants have a harder time sticking. It's a smart long-term investment for a new car, a daily driver or a classic. See [Ceramic Coating](https://oregondetail.com/ceramic-coating).

### What's the difference between a wax and a ceramic coating?

Wax or sealant sits on top of the paint and lasts around 6–8 months. A ceramic coating chemically bonds to the clear coat and lasts years. Coated cars get better protection against light scratches and contamination, strong water beading and deeper gloss. Every vehicle is polished before coating so the best finish is locked in, not imperfections.

### My headlights are yellow and foggy. Can they be fixed?

Yes. Headlight restoration starts at $50 per headlight: the failed UV coating and oxidized plastic are wet sanded away, each lens is compounded and polished, then finished with two coats of UV-resistant ceramic. Every restoration includes a 2-year warranty.
