## What Paint Correction Fixes

Paint correction levels the clear coat with a machine polisher, so light reflects evenly again instead of catching on swirls and scratches.

What it removes
- Swirl marks from automatic washes and dirty towels
- Light scratches and marring in the clear coat
- Haze and dullness that wash and wax won't fix
- Texture and deeper defects, with wet sanding on a 4-Stage

What it doesn't do
- Fill rock chips. [Paint protection film](https://oregondetail.com/paint-protection-film) helps stop new ones.
- Remove scratches that go through the clear coat
- Keep new swirls away on its own. A sealant or [ceramic coating](https://oregondetail.com/ceramic-coating) protects the finish after.

Paint correction comes first in every [ceramic coating](https://oregondetail.com/ceramic-coating) and [PPF](https://oregondetail.com/paint-protection-film) install, because a coating or film seals in whatever is underneath. ## Correction Process

Every level starts the same way. The steps in between depend on the level you choose.

1. **Hand wash.** A full hand wash to remove loose dirt and grime.
2. **Decontamination.** Clay bar treatment pulls bonded contaminants out of the paint.
3. **Wet sanding.** 4-Stage only. Levels defects and texture that polishing alone can't reach.
4. **Compound.** 2- and 4-Stage. A heavy-cut compound removes deeper scratches and swirls.
5. **Polish.** A refining polish brings back clarity and gloss. A 4-Stage adds a micro-refining jeweling polish.
6. **Protect.** A 6-month paint sealant, or upgrade to a ceramic coating.
