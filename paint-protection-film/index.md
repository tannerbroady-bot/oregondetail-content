## Installation Process

1. **Hand wash.** A full hand wash to remove loose dirt and grime.
2. **Decontamination.** Clay bar treatment pulls bonded contaminants out of the paint.
3. **Paint correction.** Swirls and scratches are polished out, so the film doesn't seal them in.
4. **Film install.** Film is fitted panel by panel inside our enclosed shop.
5. **Edge check.** Every edge and seam is inspected under shop lighting.
6. **Delivery.** Pick up your protected vehicle and we'll go over how to care for the film.

We never install film over contaminated or defective paint, and every install happens indoors.

### Which PPF package should I choose?

Standard Front covers the areas that chip first: the bumper and the front of the hood and fenders. Full Front covers those panels completely and adds the mirrors, so no film line shows. Track Pack adds the A-pillars, roof edge, rockers, lower doors, lower rear quarter panels, door edges and handle cups for highway and track driving. Full Wrap covers every painted panel.

### What is the difference between a partial hood and a full hood?

A partial hood covers the front portion of the hood and leaves a film edge partway up the panel. A full hood covers the whole panel and wraps to its edges, so there is no line to see. The same applies to partial and full fenders.

### Does PPF replace ceramic coating?

They do different jobs. Paint protection film is a physical layer that absorbs rock chips and scratches. Ceramic coating adds gloss and makes the car easier to wash, but it does not stop chips. Many vehicles get PPF on the high-impact areas and ceramic coating over the whole vehicle.

### Do you correct the paint before installing film?

Yes. Film seals in whatever is underneath it, so we wash, decontaminate and polish the paint before any film goes on.
