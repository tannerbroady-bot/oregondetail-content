## Installation Process

1. **Hand wash** — a full hand wash to remove loose dirt and grime.
2. **Decontamination** — clay bar treatment pulls bonded contaminants out of the paint.
3. **Enhancement polish** — machine polishing brings the paint to its best condition before it's locked in.
4. **Coating** — applied panel by panel for even, complete coverage.
5. **Indoor cure** — at least 12 hours of curing indoors.
6. **Delivery** — a walkthrough of coating care and the annual maintenance visits.

Coating is never applied over contaminated or defective paint, and the vehicle stays overnight at minimum.


## What Ceramic Coating Does

A ceramic coating is a liquid polymer applied by hand that cures into a bond with your factory clear coat. Unlike wax, it doesn't wash off in a few weeks.

**What it does**

- Water and road film bead up and roll off after every rain
- Tree sap, pollen and bird droppings wipe off before they etch
- Deeper gloss than wax, with UV protection through Oregon summers
- Washes take a fraction of the time

**What it doesn't do**

- Stop rock chips or deep scratches. That's [paint protection film](https://oregondetail.com/paint-protection-film), and the two pair well.
- Make paint scratch-proof. No product does.
- Hide swirls. That's why every package starts with a polish.

### Does ceramic coating prevent rock chips and scratches?

Ceramic coating increases resistance to light swirls and environmental damage, but it does not protect against rock chips or deep scratches from road debris. For impact protection, [paint protection film (PPF)](https://oregondetail.com/paint-protection-film) is the right product. Many vehicles benefit from PPF on high-impact zones combined with ceramic coating over the entire vehicle.

### What does the ceramic coating installation process look like?

Eugene and Springfield's heavy tree canopy (sap and pollen), wet winters (water spotting) and UV-heavy summers make ceramic coating a strong investment. A Level 2 coating costs less over 5 years than consistent waxing, protects better and includes annual maintenance. For drivers keeping a vehicle 3+ years, at least a Level 1 installation usually makes sense.

### How do I maintain a ceramic-coated vehicle?
