# Should You Protect Your Car's Paint Before Winter in Oregon?

*By Tanner Broady, founder of Oregon Detail Co · November 6, 2026*

Yes. If you're keeping your car through an Oregon winter, the best time to protect the paint is before the rain settles in, not after. In Eugene the wet season runs from mid-October into early May, and December alone averages close to 9 inches of rain. A layer of protection applied in the fall means road grime, de-icer and tree sap land on that layer instead of your clear coat for the next six months.

## What Oregon winters do to your paint

Our winters aren't harsh the way the Midwest's are, but they're long and wet, and that's its own problem.

### Months of wet road grime

Every rainy drive throws a film of oil, brake dust and road dirt onto the lower half of the car. When the car never fully dries, that film sits on the paint for days at a time. Over a winter it dulls the finish and works its way into the clear coat.

### De-icer on the passes and I-5

ODOT treats roads with magnesium chloride and salt to keep ice from bonding. If you drive to the mountains, over the pass or up I-5 during a cold snap, your car picks up chloride residue that keeps working on paint, wheels and the underside until it's washed off.

### Sap, needles and moss

Parking under firs and maples means sap, needles and leaves that hold water against the paint. Left long enough, sap etches and leaves stain.

### Less washing, less drying

Nobody wants to wash a car in 40-degree rain, so most cars go longer between washes in winter. That's exactly when contaminants do the most damage.

## Winter care once it's protected

- Wash every two to three weeks, and soon after any trip over the pass or through treated roads.
- Rinse the lower panels and wheel wells, where de-icer and grime collect.
- Use a pH-neutral car shampoo so you don't strip the sealant or coating.
- Get sap and bird droppings off within a few days.
- Skip automatic brush washes, which leave swirl marks in any finish.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is it too late to protect my paint once the rain has started?

No. Because we work indoors, the car is dried and prepped in a controlled space no matter what the weather is doing. Earlier is better only because the paint spends fewer weeks unprotected.

### How long does a paint sealant last in Oregon?

About 6–8 months, which is roughly one wet season. A sealant applied in October will carry the car to spring.

### Does ceramic coating help in the rain?

Yes. Coated paint sheds water and road film instead of holding it, so the car stays cleaner between washes and dirt rinses off with far less effort.

### Do Oregon roads use salt?

ODOT uses magnesium chloride and salt to treat roads for ice, mostly in colder areas and during winter storms. Wash the car, especially the lower panels and underside, after driving on treated roads.
